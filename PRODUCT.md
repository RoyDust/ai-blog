# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Inkforge serves two overlapping user groups: readers browsing a polished technical blog, and operators or authors running the content workflow behind it. Readers need fast discovery, readable article pages, search, archives, categories, tags, bookmarks, comments, and dark mode. Operators need an efficient admin workspace for drafting, publishing, reviewing comments, managing taxonomy, monitoring traffic, and running AI-assisted writing and news pipelines.

## Product Purpose

Inkforge turns source material into publishable articles and keeps the full content operation observable. Success means readers can quickly find and enjoy content, while authors and admins can move from topic discovery to AI assistance, review, publishing, and analytics without leaving the app.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, editorial. The public surface should feel like a refined reading room for thoughtful technical writing. The admin surface should feel familiar, dense, and trustworthy rather than decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-purple SaaS landing pages, overdecorated glass cards, decorative motion that slows task work, unreadable low-contrast gray text, and marketing-page scaffolding in the admin workspace. Avoid making the blog feel like a default Markdown theme with no editorial character.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize reading quality before visual novelty.
- Keep admin workflows efficient, predictable, and state-rich.
- Preserve the dual identity: editorial public site, operational product backend.
- Use AI as a visible workflow capability, not as vague decoration.
- Let visual polish reinforce trust, accessibility, and content clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public reading and admin task flows. Maintain keyboard-visible focus states, readable contrast in light and dark themes, reduced-motion fallbacks, semantic navigation landmarks, and clear empty, loading, and error states.
